# EQUENCY Roadmap — Developer Brief

## Objective

Build one dedicated section on the landing page or a dedicated `/roadmap` page explaining the future evolution of EQUENCY.

This should **not** feel like a normal SaaS feature roadmap.

The core message is:

> **Every new market gets a Mind.**

EQUENCY is not simply expanding into more asset analyzers. The goal is to expand the **Intelligence Core framework** across new markets and emerging asset classes.

Each market or asset can receive its own persistent Intelligence Core that can research, remember, observe change, build an evolving thesis, and develop with the asset over time.

---

## 1. Core Narrative

### Hero

**ROADMAP**

### Headline

> **Every new market gets a Mind.**

### Subheadline

> EQUENCY is expanding beyond newly public companies to give newly emerging assets and markets their own persistent Intelligence Core.

### Supporting Copy

> A Core does more than analyze an asset once. It continuously observes its environment, researches what matters, remembers what it learns, tracks events and evidence, and develops an evolving thesis over time.

Do not overuse the word **AI**.

Preferred vocabulary:

**Intelligence → Core → Mind → Research → Memory → Thesis**

Not:

**AI → Analysis → Prediction**

# 3. Roadmap Phases

Use exactly **4 phases**.

## PHASE 01 — Newly Tokenized Stocks

### Give every newly tokenized stock its own Intelligence Core.

When a company enters the onchain economy through a tokenized stock, EQUENCY can initialize a Core for that asset.

The Core should understand both sides of the asset.

### Underlying Company

- fundamentals
- SEC filings
- earnings
- corporate events
- news
- institutional activity

### Onchain Representation

- token price
- liquidity
- volume
- holders
- market activity
- issuer / backing
- oracle / pricing
- DeFi integrations

### Short Description

> The Core connects the underlying company with its onchain representation, creating a persistent intelligence layer that evolves as both markets change.

**Label:** `COMPANY + ONCHAIN INTELLIGENCE`

---

## PHASE 02 — Newly Launched Stock-Paired Tokens

### Give every qualifying stock-paired token its own Mind.

Focus specifically on tokens launched with a **stock or tokenized stock as the pair**, particularly within the Pons ecosystem.

This creates a unique intelligence problem because the Core needs to understand **two connected markets at the same time**.

```text
STOCK / TOKENIZED STOCK
          ↕
      ONCHAIN TOKEN
```

The Core can understand:

- token and contract
- liquidity
- holders
- volume
- price movement
- LP activity
- deployer
- social narrative
- underlying stock
- relationship between both markets

### Short Description

> These Cores understand markets across both sides of the pair, connecting onchain behavior with the underlying stock market.

### Strong Supporting Line

> **One asset. Two markets. One Mind.**

**Label:** `CROSS-MARKET INTELLIGENCE`

---

## PHASE 03 — Private / Pre-IPO

### Give private companies intelligence before they become public.

This phase is inspired by the emerging pre-IPO market, but EQUENCY's role is the **intelligence layer**, not simply tokenization.

The Private Market Core begins understanding a company before its public-market lifecycle starts.

The Core can research:

- funding history
- investors
- valuation
- products
- competitors
- partnerships
- hiring
- patents
- executive activity
- market positioning
- relevant private-market developments

### Short Description

> A Private Market Core builds intelligence before the public market exists, allowing the same intelligence layer to evolve with a company as it moves from private to public.

Show the lifecycle:

```text
PRIVATE
   ↓
PRE-IPO
   ↓
IPO
   ↓
PUBLIC
```

Supporting line:

> **One Core. A continuous intelligence lifecycle.**

The Core does not necessarily disappear when a company becomes public. Its intelligence can evolve with the company.

**Label:** `PRE-MARKET INTELLIGENCE`

---

## PHASE 04 — Newly Tokenized Commodities

### Give newly tokenized commodities their own Intelligence Core.

This is a **conditional future expansion**. Do not imply that all commodity markets are already available or that this phase is already implemented.

Potential examples:

- Gold
- Silver
- Oil
- Commodity baskets

The Core should understand two layers.

### Underlying Commodity

- supply and demand
- macro conditions
- inventory
- futures
- rates
- geopolitical events
- commodity-specific catalysts

### Tokenized Asset

- token price
- backing
- reserves
- issuer
- mint / redemption
- liquidity
- holders
- premium / discount
- onchain activity

### Short Description

> When meaningful tokenized commodity markets emerge, EQUENCY can extend the Intelligence Core framework to understand both the underlying commodity and its onchain representation.

**Label:** `REAL-WORLD + ONCHAIN INTELLIGENCE`

# 9. UI / Design Direction

Follow the existing EQUENCY visual DNA:

- deep navy / near-black background
- restrained blue primary accent
- subtle orange secondary accent
- thin borders
- thin connecting lines
- premium financial-terminal aesthetic
- data-dense but breathable
- sophisticated typography
- subtle wireframe/network globe
- minimal glow
- refined, technical, expensive feel

Avoid:

- excessive neon
- cyberpunk
- cartoonish visuals
- generic AI aesthetics
- robot heads
- AI brain graphics
- excessive particles
- glassmorphism overload
- meme aesthetics

### Roadmap Cards

Do not make four large SaaS-style feature cards.

Each phase should feel like a **node in an intelligence network**.

Example:

```text
01
NEWLY TOKENIZED STOCKS

[ subtle network / globe visualization ]

Every newly tokenized stock
gets an Intelligence Core.

COMPANY + ONCHAIN
INTELLIGENCE
```

Connect each phase with thin lines or network paths.

---

# 10. Animation

Animations should be subtle and purposeful.

Good examples:

- node appears
- connection line gradually forms
- Core status has a small pulse
- wireframe globe moves very slowly
- data points appear subtly
- phase becomes active while scrolling

Avoid:

- huge glowing animations
- excessive particles
- cyberpunk effects
- fake "AI thinking" animations
- random fake numbers
- fake research logs

The page should feel like **real intelligence infrastructure**, not an AI gimmick.

# Final Developer Instruction

The most important requirement is that this page **must not look like a list of future analysis features**.

The visitor should leave the page understanding one simple idea:

> **EQUENCY is building a network of persistent Minds for new markets.**

A newly emerging asset enters the ecosystem.

**A Core is born.**

It gets a mission, researches the market, collects evidence, builds memory, develops a thesis, observes change, and evolves with the asset.

The four roadmap phases are the first markets where this Intelligence Core framework will expand:

**Newly Tokenized Stocks → Stock-Paired Tokens on Pons → Private / Pre-IPO → Newly Tokenized Commodities.**

These are **roadmap targets**, not necessarily currently implemented capabilities. The UI/copy must not imply that these future phases are already live.
