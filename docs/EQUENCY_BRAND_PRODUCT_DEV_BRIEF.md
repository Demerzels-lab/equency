# EQUENCY --- Brand, Product Narrative & UI/UX Implementation Brief

## 0. Purpose

This document is the implementation brief for the next iteration of
**EQUENCY**.

The goal is not to make the current website "look more branded" while
leaving the product experience unchanged.

The goal is to make the **product itself communicate the EQUENCY
thesis**:

> **Every newly public company gets an Intelligence Core.**

EQUENCY should feel like a living intelligence layer for newly public
companies, not a conventional IPO tracker, stock screener, AI research
chatbot, or generic AI investment dashboard.

The current implementation already contains many of the right building
blocks. The screen recording shows:

-   `Tracking IPOs`
-   `Allocating IPOs`
-   `Strategy`
-   `Core`
-   `Freshly Public`
-   `EQUENCY IN NUMBERS`
-   `Evidence Graph`
-   `Reasoning Engine`
-   `Deterministic Score`
-   `The newly-public universe`
-   company/IPO tables
-   Intelligence Core terminology
-   strategy and vault navigation

These should **not be discarded**.

They should be reorganized and rewritten so the user immediately
understands the deeper product model:

> **A company becomes public. EQUENCY gives it a mind. That mind
> continuously observes, researches, forms a thesis, and can eventually
> connect that intelligence to strategy and capital.**

The public repository already has a working implementation with
frontend, contracts, docs, scripts, and source code. This brief is
therefore an **evolution brief**, not a request to rebuild EQUENCY from
zero.

# 2. What EQUENCY Is NOT

Do not position EQUENCY primarily as:

-   an AI stock screener
-   an AI stock picker
-   an AI Bloomberg
-   a stock prediction engine
-   an IPO calendar
-   a financial chatbot
-   an autonomous trading bot
-   a generic AI agent platform
-   a portfolio tracker

These descriptions reduce EQUENCY to one feature.

The intended mental model is:

> **Company → Intelligence Core → Thesis → Strategy → Capital**

The website should make this progression visually and conceptually
obvious.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 3. The AGENCY-Inspired Concept, Without Copying AGENCY

The strategic inspiration is the idea that an asset can have its own
persistent intelligence.

AGENCY's mental model can be summarized as:

> Every coin gets a mind.

EQUENCY should establish its own version:

> **Every newly public company gets a mind.**

The important difference is that EQUENCY is not a clone of AGENCY.

### AGENCY-like idea

Asset → Mind → Research → Strategy → Action

### EQUENCY

Company → Intelligence Core → Research → Thesis → Strategy → Capital

The EQUENCY version should feel more financial, analytical,
evidence-driven, and purpose-built for newly public companies.

Do not copy AGENCY terminology, visuals, layouts, or exact UI patterns.

Borrow the **product philosophy**, not the surface design.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 4. The New Mental Model

When a user visits EQUENCY, they should understand these five ideas in
order:

## 01 --- A company becomes public

A newly public company enters the EQUENCY universe.

## 02 --- EQUENCY gives it an Intelligence Core

The company is no longer just a ticker in a database.

It has a persistent intelligence layer attached to it.

## 03 --- The Intelligence Core researches

It continuously observes:

-   SEC filings
-   company information
-   earnings
-   fundamentals
-   price
-   volume
-   options
-   institutional activity
-   news
-   X/social narrative
-   company announcements
-   corporate events
-   liquidity
-   market behavior

## 04 --- The Intelligence Core develops a thesis

The Core should be able to answer:

> What is happening?

> Why does it matter?

> What evidence supports this?

> What changed?

> What could invalidate the thesis?

## 05 --- Intelligence can become strategy and capital

The final product loop is:

**Intelligence → Strategy → Capital**

The user remains in control of capital deployment.

# 28. FAQ Rewrite

The existing FAQ is already addressing important trust questions.

Keep the FAQ but rewrite it around the new product model.

## What does an Intelligence Core actually do?

> An Intelligence Core continuously monitors and researches a newly
> public company using public filings, market data, company information,
> news, social signals, and other supported sources. It turns that
> evidence into structured intelligence and an evolving thesis.

## Where does the data come from?

> EQUENCY uses supported public and market data sources. Each material
> intelligence output should retain source references and timestamps so
> users can inspect the underlying evidence.

## Does the Core make investment decisions?

> The Core produces intelligence and strategy recommendations. Capital
> deployment remains subject to the user's strategy, risk constraints,
> and approval settings.

## Does the Core run continuously?

> The Core operates through event-driven and scheduled research cycles.
> It does not need to generate fake activity when there is nothing
> meaningful to investigate.

## Is the Core just an AI model?

> No. The Core combines structured data, deterministic signals, research
> workflows, persistent memory, evidence tracking, and AI reasoning.

## Can the thesis change?

> Yes. A Core's thesis is designed to evolve when new evidence changes
> the underlying picture.

## Is this financial advice?

> EQUENCY provides research, intelligence, and strategy tooling. Users
> are responsible for their own investment decisions.

# 39. UI State System

Create a consistent state language.

### ACTIVE STATES

**AWAKE** Core has recently activated.

**OBSERVING** Monitoring relevant data.

**RESEARCHING** Executing a research task.

**RE-EVALUATING** New evidence is being compared with the existing
thesis.

**THESIS SHIFT** The current thesis has materially changed.

### INACTIVE STATES

**MONITORING** No high-priority research event is currently active.

**IDLE** No meaningful research task is currently running.

Never use:

> THINKING...

as the only state.

It is vague and often implies fake activity.

# 48. Important Product Rule

The Intelligence Core should not be a chatbot first.

A chatbot asks:

> "What do you want to know about XYZ?"

The EQUENCY Core should proactively surface:

> "Here is what changed."

> "Here is what I am investigating."

> "Here is the evidence."

> "Here is how the thesis changed."

That distinction is critical.

EQUENCY is not:

> ChatGPT for stocks.

It is:

> **Persistent intelligence attached to newly public companies.**
